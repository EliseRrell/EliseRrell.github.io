About me

I think in systems.
I design for people.

I'm a UX designer turned developer with a genuine curiosity about how things work, both from the user's perspective and under the hood. During my studies I discovered something that most teams haven't figured out yet: the best digital products come from people who understand both sides of the equation.

That's the role I want to play. Whether it means sketching user flows in Figma or writing clean C# APIs, I care about making the whole experience right, not just the part that's technically "my job".

  • I ask "why" before "how"
  • I write code I'd be happy to read in six months
  • I believe accessibility is non-negotiable
  • I thrive in teams that give and take feedback openly

How I work

Understand Research, interviews & defining the real problem
Design Wireframes, flows & high-fidelity prototypes
Build Clean, maintainable code that matches the intent
Iterate Test, refine & improve based on real feedback
